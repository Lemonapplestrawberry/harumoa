Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-07-06 · 한국어
At a glance
Harumoa has no sign-up. All data — including your baby's photos — is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, there are no ads or tracking tools, and no information is ever shared with third parties.

1. Information we handle and where it lives

ItemStored inPurpose
Baby's name and date of birthOn deviceDay counter (D+n), reminder text
Daily photosOn deviceGrowth record and timelapse playback
App settings (reminder time, etc.)On deviceApp functionality

Harumoa does not collect accounts, emails, or phone numbers. Subscription payments are processed by Apple; Harumoa never sees your payment details.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera — used only for your daily photo.
  • Photo library (read) — used only when importing past photos.
  • Photo library (save) — used only if you enable "Also save to Photos".
  • Notifications — used only for the daily reminder.

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature. Declining a permission does not affect the rest of the app.

3. Retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes the data. (Copies saved to your photo library via "Also save to Photos" remain in your library.)

4. Children's privacy

Harumoa's users are parents and guardians (adults). Baby photos are taken and kept by the guardian and never leave the guardian's device.

5. Third parties and advertising

None. Harumoa uses no ad networks and no analytics/tracking SDKs.

6. Future changes

When optional cloud backup (a subscription feature) launches, photos of users who enable it may be stored encrypted on our servers. This policy will be updated and announced in the app before that feature ships.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: harumoa.help@gmail.com
